# Presets and settings

Most people pick a preset and never touch anything else. If you want more control, every value a preset sets can be changed, and doing so switches you to **Custom**.

## The presets

| Preset | Target | Max boost | Max reduction | Reaction speed | Limiter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Night** | 52 % | +9 dB | −30 dB | 0.7 (quicker) | High |
| **Normal** (default) | 68 % | +6 dB | −24 dB | 0.5 | Medium |
| **Cinema** | 82 % | +4 dB | −12 dB | 0.35 (smoother) | Low |
| **Custom** | your choice | your choice | your choice | your choice | your choice |

- **Night** holds everything low and lifts dialogue more, for watching while others sleep.
- **Normal** evens out everyday viewing.
- **Cinema** lets the film breathe and only steps in for the biggest swings.

## In the popup

| Setting | Range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target volume | 20 to 100 % | From "Whisper" to "Max cinema". Use the dial, the slider or the − and + buttons. |
| Profile | Night, Normal, Cinema, Custom | Changing any tuning value selects Custom. |
| Always on for this site | on or off | Asks for permission for that one site. |
| Pause for 15 min | | Leaves sound untouched, then resumes. |
| Master switch | on or off | Also available as <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>. |

## On the settings page

Open it from the slider icon in the popup.

| Setting | Range | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reaction speed | Smooth to Instant | Smooth keeps changes gentle; Instant reacts the moment a scene gets loud. |
| Maximum boost | 0 to +12 dB | How much quiet scenes may be turned up. High values can lift background noise. |
| Maximum reduction | 0 to −30 dB | How much loud scenes may be turned down. |
| Loud-peak limiter | Low, Medium, High | Catches sudden peaks. Thresholds −1, −3 and −6 dB. |
| Theme | Match system, Dark, Light | The popup and settings page follow it. |
| Sites | list | Sites where Volume Guard runs automatically. Pause or remove any of them. |
| Keyboard shortcut | | Opens the browser's shortcut settings so you can change it. |

**Reset tuning to defaults** brings back the Normal values.

## Where settings are stored

Settings live in your browser's extension storage. If you use your browser's sync, the browser may copy them to your other devices. Nothing is sent anywhere else. See [Privacy and permissions](/docs/privacy-and-permissions).

## Tips

- **Hiss in quiet scenes?** Lower the maximum boost.
- **Music sounds flat?** Raise the target or lower the maximum reduction.
- **Volume seems to breathe around speech?** Move reaction speed towards Smooth.
