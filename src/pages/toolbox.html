The toolbox

Every part, in your hands.

Volume Guard has nine parts. Scroll through them one by one, and use the controls in the corner: move the target and watch the leveller follow, or bypass it to hear (and see) the raw soundtrack.

Scroll through the parts
Part 01

Target dial

One number: how loud you want it.

The dial goes from 20% (a whisper) to 100% (cinema). At 68% the target is −14 LUFS, and every percent moves it by 0.25 LU. The teal lines on the board are your target; try the slider.

Range
20–100%
Default
68% · −14 LUFS
Step
0.25 LU per percent
Part 02

Leveller

Down fast, up slowly.

Volume Guard measures loudness many times a second and moves the volume towards your target. Loud scenes come down quickly; quiet scenes rise gently, so nothing pumps. Within 1 dB of the target it leaves the sound alone.

Measures
Perceived loudness, on your device
Dead band
1 dB
Behaviour
Fast down, slow up
Part 03

Boost cap

Lifts the quiet, never the hiss.

Quiet dialogue is lifted, but only so far. Boosting a whisper all the way would also blow up background noise, so each preset has a cap: some quiet bars stay below the line on purpose.

Night
Up to 9 dB
Normal
Up to 6 dB
Cinema
Up to 4 dB
Part 04

Limiter

Catches what is too fast to level.

A gunshot or a door slam is over before any leveller can react. A safety limiter sits at the end of the chain and rounds off those sharp peaks (the amber line on the board) before they reach your ears.

Acts on
Sudden peaks
Strength
Low, medium or high per preset
Part 05

Bypass

Hear the original in one click.

Curious what the movie sounds like without Volume Guard? Bypass passes the sound through untouched, without reloading the video. Flip the Bypass switch in the corner to compare.

Part 06

Pause

Fifteen minutes off, then back on its own.

Watching a concert where the swings are the point? Pause Volume Guard for 15 minutes from the popup. It comes back by itself, so you never forget to turn it on again.

Pause length
15 minutes
Part 07

Per-site

On where you watch, off everywhere else.

Choose "Always on for this site" and Volume Guard asks your browser for access to that one site only. The front row of the board is a site you turned on; the back row is one you did not. Remove access any time in Settings.

Part 08 · Experimental

Compatibility

For protected streams, with honest limits.

Copy-protected video cannot be adjusted directly, so Compatibility mode levels the whole tab's audio instead. It asks for extra permissions only when you turn it on, may add a small delay to the sound, and has not yet been verified on real protected sites.

Status
Experimental
Asks for
Tab capture, when you turn it on
Trade-off
A small audio delay
Part 09

Shortcut

On and off without opening anything.

Press Alt+Shift+V to turn Volume Guard on or off. On this page the same keys flip the Bypass switch, so you can try it now.

Alt+Shift+V
