Support

Something off?

Most problems have a quick fix. Try the steps below first. If that does not help, tell us on GitHub and we will take a look.

The volume does not change

  1. Make sure the video is playing. Volume Guard starts once there is sound, and the status shows Ready — start playback until then.
  2. Open the popup and check the master switch at the top is on, and that it is not paused.
  3. Look at the status line. On target means the sound is already at your level, so there is nothing to correct.
  4. If you just installed or updated the extension, reload the video page once.

The popup says the video is protected or blocked

Copy-protected and some cross-origin videos cannot be adjusted directly by any extension. Volume Guard leaves them untouched on purpose, so playback is never muted by mistake. You can try the experimental Compatibility mode from the same popup.

It does not start by itself on a site

Volume Guard runs on a tab after you open its popup there. To have it start automatically, turn on Always on for this site and accept the browser's permission prompt for that site.

It sounds wrong

  • Hiss rises in quiet scenes: lower Maximum boost on the settings page.
  • Music sounds flat: raise the target or lower Maximum reduction.
  • The volume seems to breathe around speech: move Reaction speed towards Smooth.
  • Sound is behind the picture: this can happen in Compatibility mode. Turn it off for that tab.

Start fresh

On the settings page, Reset tuning to defaults brings back the Normal preset. To remove access to a site, delete it from the site list there, or from your browser's extension settings.

Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub. These details help fix it quickly:

  • Your browser and its version (for example Chrome 131 or Edge 131) and your operating system.
  • The site, and whether the video was a film, a series or a short clip.
  • What the popup's status line said.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • Whether Compatibility mode was on.

Please do not include personal information. For a security problem, do not post details publicly: open an issue asking for a private contact.
