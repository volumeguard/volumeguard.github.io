How it works

Every movie is too loud and too quiet.

  1. Pick your level

    Set a target volume once, or choose Night, Normal or Cinema. That is your line.

  2. Press play

    While the video plays, Volume Guard measures how loud it is, many times a second, on your device.

  3. Touch nothing

    Loud scenes come down fast, quiet ones come up gently. Your hand stays on the popcorn.

Control room

Two taps. That is the whole app.

Open the popup, set your level, done. The dial shows your target, the meters show the sound coming in and the balanced sound going out, and a status line tells you exactly what it is doing.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

All questions→
Is it free?

Yes. Volume Guard is open source and has no ads, accounts or paid tiers.

Which sites does it work on?

Most sites that play video in the browser, including embedded players. Copy-protected streams cannot be adjusted directly; there Volume Guard leaves the sound untouched and tells you why.

What about Netflix and other protected streams?

An experimental Compatibility mode can level a whole tab's audio. It asks for extra permission only when you turn it on and may add a small delay. It is still being tested on real sites.

Does it see what I watch?

No. It never reads titles, page content or history, and it makes no network requests. Audio is measured in memory and never recorded.

Where can I get it?

It is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. You can build it from the source code today; the docs walk you through it.
