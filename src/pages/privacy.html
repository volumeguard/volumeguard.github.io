Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Volume Guard analyses the sound of the videos you watch on your device only. This policy covers the browser extension and this website.

The extension

  • Audio is measured in memory to decide how loud to play it. It is never recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • The extension makes no network requests and contains no analytics, telemetry or advertising.
  • It stores only your settings (target volume, preset, limits, reaction speed, limiter strength and theme) and the hostnames of sites where you turned it on, using your browser's extension storage. If you use your browser's sync feature, the browser may sync these settings between your devices.
  • It does not read page content, video titles or your browsing history. A site's hostname is used only to apply your per-site choice.
  • Compatibility mode (only if you turn it on for a tab, after a permission prompt) captures that tab's audio so it can be levelled. The audio stays in memory on your device, is played back to you, and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Turning it off, closing the tab or reloading stops the capture.
  • Optional site access is requested per site, only when you choose Always on for this site, and you can remove it at any time from the extension's settings page or your browser's extension settings.

This website

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Everything it needs (fonts, scripts, images and 3D scenes) is served from this site. It loads nothing from other servers.
  • The optional Sound demo is generated in your browser while you listen. Nothing is downloaded or recorded, and it never plays unless you press the button.
  • Your browser may remember small preferences locally (for example which page you came from, to play the page transition). This stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.
  • The site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, which, like any web host, processes standard request data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.
  • The site has no forms and does not ask you for any information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date. The extension's behaviour can always be checked in its source code, which is open to inspect.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page. More detail on permissions is in Privacy and permissions.
